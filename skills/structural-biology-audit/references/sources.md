## Verified conflicts and update boundaries

- **FSC criteria:** Read the Rosenthal–Henderson 2003 derivation of 0.143 alongside the information/statistical criteria of van Heel–Schatz 2005. State applicability in terms of half-map independence, shell sampling, masking and the target statistic. A community reporting convention neither establishes that an alternative criterion has been disproved nor makes a threshold evidence of local correctness. See `rh2003`, `vanheel2005`, `kleywegt2024` and [cryo-em.md](cryo-em.md).
- **X-ray cutoff and coordinate precision:** R-free, CC½ and paired refinement answer complementary questions; a signal threshold is not a universal dataset cutoff. Apply the Cruickshank erratum to original Eqs. (30), (49), symbols and cross-references before reusing the formulas. See [xray.md](xray.md).
- **Crystal versus solution assembly:** PISA inference and condition-matched solution measurements address different states. Prioritize direct evidence for the claimed state, checking construct, concentration, salt, ligand and temperature. Krissinel 2011 gives Kd ≥ 100 μM; the PDF extraction incorrectly rendered μ as m, and the unit was checked against official IUCr text. See [biological-context.md](biological-context.md).
- **Prediction versus experiment:** A confident prediction can propose an alternative; it does not override an experimental map by confidence alone. Assess experimental data quality too. Register flags are candidate screens, not error posterior probabilities or automatic verdicts. See [predicted-models.md](predicted-models.md) and [model-validation.md](model-validation.md).
- **AF3 Addendum:** The verified notice announces release of inference code; it does not revise the accuracy results. The “Corrections & amendments” category does not invalidate all original metrics. Both directions of the DOI relationship are retained in JSON `updates`.
